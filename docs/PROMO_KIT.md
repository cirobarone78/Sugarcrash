## 4) Golden rules (don't get banned or blacklisted)

1. **Don't drop the same link everywhere.** Vary the text; identical mass posts
   trigger spam filters and can get the domain blacklisted (which also hurts SEO).
2. **Read each community's self-promo rules** and post only where allowed;
   participate genuinely first.
3. **Lead with value, not just a link** — ask for feedback, describe a feature,
   share a specific room.
4. **Respect 18+ / geo / ToS everywhere.** Never target or reach minors.
5. **Quality over quantity** — a few good listings + real community posts beat
   100 spammy drops.
